DoseShift: Pill Reminder App · version —

Your next dose — no manual time math.

DoseShift is an Android pill reminder app for schedules where the next dose depends on when you actually took the previous one.

Download DoseShift: Pill Reminder App for Android

The APK download starts immediately. Installation is available on Android only.

What the app does

Tap Taken now after a dose and DoseShift moves the next reminder by the interval you configured. You do not need to calculate a new time by hand when a dose is late.

Who it is for

DoseShift supports one medication plan such as “every N hours after a dose,” including a family member setting it up. It is a reminder, not medical advice, and does not replace a clinician's instructions.

Install from outside Google Play

DoseShift is currently distributed directly. Android will show its normal warning about installing an app from this source. Allow installation from the browser, confirm the install, and disable that permission afterwards if you prefer.

Privacy

No account is required. Medication name, plan, marks, and notes are kept by the app on the phone. The website and app use a random identifier for download and update counters; it is not connected to dose data. Read the privacy notice for details about local data, cookies, technical logs, and feedback. Short answers about the app are in the FAQ.

Start using DoseShift

  1. Open the app after installing the APK. Set up reminders appears immediately.
  2. Enter the medication name, interval in hours, maximum doses per day and first planned time from the prescription. DoseShift does not choose the interval or daily maximum.
  3. Tap Enable reminders and allow notifications. If the main screen shows Set up, use it to complete the required Android setting.
  4. After a dose, tap Taken now in the app or Taken in the notification. The next reminder shifts from the marked time. After the daily maximum, it moves to the next day's starting time.

When plans change

  • In 15 minutes postpones a due reminder. Before it is due, the button is Move 15 minutes later.
  • Undo last mark restores the previous schedule while no later change has been made.
  • More contains Taken at another time, Correct last dose time and Until tomorrow. The last option pauses today's remaining reminders only; it resumes tomorrow.
  • Change plan recalculates future reminders and keeps history. Finish course stops reminders and keeps history; use Start a new course to begin again.
  • No mark in history means the app has no confirmation. It does not prove a dose was missed.

Language, reminder check and updates

Russian is selected only when the phone's primary language is Russian; every other phone language uses English. In Settings → Language, select Phone language, English or Русский. Settings also contains reminder sound, Android notification settings and Test reminder; the test does not change the plan. Delivery depends on Android permissions, sound volume, precise-alarm access and background restrictions.

Use Check for updates at the bottom of the app. When a new release is available, Download update appears. Installing an update over the existing app keeps local data unless the app is removed.
