Enable DoseShift reminders on Android

Reminder delivery depends on each phone's settings. After you create a schedule, test it on your own device: DoseShift cannot override disabled notifications, silent mode, or Android background restrictions.

Quick check

  1. Open Settings in DoseShift.
  2. Tap Notification settings and check that DoseShift notifications and its reminder channel are enabled.
  3. Tap Test reminder. This test does not change the schedule.
  4. Check volume, Do Not Disturb, and the selected reminder sound.

If DoseShift shows “Set up”

Open the hint on the main screen. Android may ask you to allow precise reminders or remove a background restriction. These settings reduce delays, but their names and locations vary by phone maker.

If a reminder is still missing

  • Check that the schedule is active and the next reminder time has not passed.
  • Check that the course was not finished and Until tomorrow was not selected.
  • Open the app after restarting the phone and check the next reminder time.
  • On phones with aggressive battery saving, allow DoseShift to run in the background in system battery settings.
The reminder test checks notification visibility and sound. It is not medical advice and does not change your plan.
