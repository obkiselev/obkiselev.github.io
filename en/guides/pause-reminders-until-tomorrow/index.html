Pause Pill Reminders Until Tomorrow

In DoseShift, Until Tomorrow temporarily stops only the remaining reminders for today. The plan resumes automatically the next day. Use it to pause reminders, not to change a prescription.

Pause Today’s Remaining Reminders

  1. Open the DoseShift main screen.
  2. Tap More.
  3. Choose Until Tomorrow.
  4. Read the confirmation and confirm the action.

The screen shows Paused Until Tomorrow. No new reminders for this plan will be shown today.

Important: pausing does not mark a dose as taken, change the last-dose time, or change a prescription. For a question about what to do with a dose, follow the clinician's instructions.

What Happens Tomorrow

DoseShift continues the plan the next day. This action does not finish a course or remove the history kept on the phone.

To Stop Reminders Permanently

In More, choose Finish Course. This stops reminders and keeps local history. Create a new course when you want to track a plan again.

If the Last-Dose Time Needs Correction

Do not use the pause to correct a time. Read how to correct the last dose time.
