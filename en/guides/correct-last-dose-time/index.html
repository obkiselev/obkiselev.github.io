Correct the Last Dose Time

If you marked a dose at the wrong time, DoseShift lets you correct the most recent mark and calculate the next reminder from the chosen time.

Correct the Most Recent Mark

  1. On the main screen, tap More.
  2. Choose Correct Last Dose Time.
  3. Enter the actual time of the most recent dose and confirm.

The app updates the last mark and calculates the next reminder using the interval in your plan.

Example: with a 4-hour interval, changing the time from 08:00 to 08:17 moves the next reminder to 12:17. The interval and daily limit remain the values entered during setup.

When the Button Is Available

Correction is available while the last mark can still be undone. If another action has already happened, the button may not be available: the app does not rewrite history after later changes.

Do Not Confuse It with Other Actions

  • Undo Last Mark restores the previous state while that is available.
  • Taken at Another Time records a dose at another time.
  • Move 15 Minutes Later postpones an upcoming reminder; it does not correct a past mark.

These actions describe how the app works and are not advice on when to take medication.
