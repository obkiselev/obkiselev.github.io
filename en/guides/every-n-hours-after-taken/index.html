Set a Reminder Every N Hours After a Dose Is Taken

DoseShift supports one schedule where the next dose depends on the actual time of the previous dose, not a fixed clock time. It does not prescribe medication or choose an interval: use the plan from your clinician.

Set Up the Schedule

  1. Open DoseShift and choose Set up reminders.
  2. Enter medication name, Interval in hours, Maximum doses per day, and First planned time.
  3. Tap Enable reminders and allow Android notifications.

After a Dose

Tap Taken now in the app or Taken in the notification. DoseShift calculates the next reminder from the marked time using the configured interval. For example, with a 4-hour interval, a mark at 08:17 moves the next reminder to 12:17.

Important: this explains the app, not when to take medication. The interval and daily maximum always come from the prescription.

When Plans Change

  • In 15 minutes postpones a reminder that is due.
  • More → Taken at another time records a past dose time and recalculates the next reminder.
  • More → Until tomorrow stops today's remaining reminders only and resumes tomorrow.
  • Finish course stops reminders and keeps history on the phone.
