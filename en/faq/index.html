DoseShift: Pill Reminder App

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about the current DoseShift release. The app helps with one interval-based plan and does not replace a clinician's instructions.

Does DoseShift Choose the Interval or Number of Doses?

No. You enter the interval, daily limit, and first planned time from the prescription. The app does not provide medical advice.

Which Schedules Does DoseShift Support?

One active plan where the next dose is due a chosen number of hours after the actual mark for the previous dose. It is not an app for complex multi-medication schedules.

What Happens After You Tap Taken Now?

The next reminder moves by the configured interval from the marked time. After the daily limit, the next reminder moves to the next day's starting time.

What Does Until Tomorrow Do?

After confirmation, it pauses only today's remaining reminders. The plan resumes tomorrow; it does not change a prescription.

Where Is Data Stored?

The medication name, plan, notes, and history are kept locally in the app. The core function does not require an account or cloud sync. Read the privacy notice for details.

Why Might a Reminder Not Arrive on Time?

Reminder delivery depends on notification permission, volume, precise-alarm access, and Android background restrictions. Settings includes a test reminder that does not change the plan.
